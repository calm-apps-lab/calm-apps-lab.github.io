Quran Nest: Surahs for Kids

Privacy Policy · effective November 21, 2026 (formerly Dua Nest)

Quran Nest helps children learn short surahs and a bedtime routine with the Quran. It is made for children and their parents, and it is built so that it does not need to know anything about them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for a name, age, email, location, photos, contacts or microphone.

What the app downloads

The recitation of Shaykh Mahmoud Khalil al-Husary is not stored inside the app. When a child presses Listen, the app downloads:

  • a small list of verses and word timings from our website (calm-apps-lab.github.io, hosted by GitHub), refreshed when the phone is online;
  • the recitation of that verse from the audio server used by Quran.com and Quran Foundation.

These are ordinary downloads. They send no name, account or device identifier, and no cookies. Like any website, these servers can see the phone's internet (IP) address when a file is requested; we do not receive it and do not ask them for it.

If a parent turns on "Save recitation on this phone", the recitation is kept in the app's own private storage so it plays without internet. It cannot be opened by other apps. A parent can remove it at any time in the parent corner, and deleting the app deletes it.

What stays on the device

Stars, learned surahs, favourites, the chosen language and the bedtime streak are saved only in the app's own storage on the phone or tablet. Nobody else, including us, can see them. Deleting the app deletes them. A parent can also reset progress in the parent corner.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children. The parent corner is placed behind a grown-ups question.

Purchases

The app is free and has no purchases. If a future version offers an optional subscription for parents, payments will be handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies, and this page will be updated first.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
