Dua Nest: Duas for Kids

Privacy Policy · effective November 7, 2026

Dua Nest helps children learn a bedtime routine and everyday duas. It is made for children and their parents, and it is built so that it does not need to know anything about them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for a name, age, email, location, photos, contacts or microphone. It works offline.

What stays on the device

Stars, learned duas, favourites and the bedtime streak are saved only in the app's own storage on the phone or tablet. Nobody else, including us, can see them. Deleting the app deletes them. A parent can also reset progress in the app's parent corner.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children. Links that leave the app and any purchase screens are placed behind a parent check, so a child cannot open them alone.

Purchases

A future version may offer an optional subscription for parents. Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see card details or the parent's account. The app only learns whether the subscription is active.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
