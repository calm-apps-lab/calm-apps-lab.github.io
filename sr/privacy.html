Spades Reunion

Privacy Policy · effective October 28, 2026

Spades Reunion is a card game you play on your own device. We built it so that it does not need to know anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not request internet access and does not send any data off your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings, career progress, Club Cup results, Deal of the Day record and an unfinished game are saved in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. Only the game can read them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
