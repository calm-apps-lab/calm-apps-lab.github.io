Dua Day: Kids Daily Duas

Privacy Policy · effective November 18, 2026

Dua Day helps children learn everyday duas and short surahs. It is made for children and their parents, and it is built so that it does not need to know anything about them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use location, photos, contacts, camera or microphone. It works offline.

What stays on the device

A parent may type the child’s first name so the friends can greet them. That name, the chosen language and settings, stars, stickers and learned duas are saved only in the app’s own storage on the phone or tablet. Nothing is sent anywhere; nobody else, including us, can see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Reminders

If a parent allows notifications, the app schedules gentle reminders on the device itself (wake-up, lunch, bedtime). No push server is involved. They can be switched off in “For parents” or in the phone’s settings.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children. Settings, links that leave the app and any purchase screens are placed behind a grown-up check, so a child cannot open them alone.

Purchases

A future version may offer an optional one-time purchase for parents. Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see card details or the parent’s account. The app only learns whether the purchase exists.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
