Sudoku Pause is a logic puzzle app by CalmAppsLab. We do not run any servers for it and we never receive your personal data.

What stays on your device

Your progress, statistics, awards, solved pictures and settings are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. Nobody else, including us, can see them. "Reset all progress" in Settings or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner in free play, a full-screen ad after some finished puzzles, and short videos you can choose to watch for a hint. Never in the middle of a puzzle and never in the daily set. To show ads, Google receives technical data from your device such as the IP address, an advertising identifier where the system allows it, the device model and how ads are viewed. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the app first shows Google's consent form; you can choose non-personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time under Settings, "Privacy choices". On iPhone the app does not ask to track you, so Google does not receive the iPhone advertising identifier. Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases

Sudoku Pause Plus and "Remove ads" are bought through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your name or payment details. To know what you have bought, the app uses RevenueCat. It receives a random ID created by the app (not your name, email or Apple/Google account), your purchases and subscription status from the store, the app version and the country of your store account. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Reminders and sharing

A daily reminder, if you switch it on, is created and shown by your phone; nothing is sent to anyone. When you share your result, your phone's normal share menu opens with a short text; you decide what to send and to whom.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Your rights

You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it. For data held by Google or RevenueCat you can use the choices described above or contact us; we will help you reach them.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
