Try the pass calculatorEpic, Epic Local, Ikon or Ikon Base: which one pays off for your season?

A season pass costs $849 to $1,449. Whether it pays off depends on how many days you actually ski, where, and on which days. Skiworth will keep count for you: it notices when you are at a resort, logs the day at that resort's window price, and shows what your pass has saved so far and what each day really cost.

What the app should do

  • Log ski days by itself at 75+ Epic and Ikon resorts in the US. You only confirm when it is unsure.
  • Value each day at the real window price, weekday or weekend, and show your cost per day.
  • Count limited days (Ikon 7 days, Ikon Base 5 days, Epic Local's 10 days at Vail and Beaver Creek) and warn you before you run out.
  • Remind you of blackout dates before you drive up.
  • A season recap to share. No account, no ads, your locations stay on your phone.
