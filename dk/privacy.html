Durak 3D: World Tour

Privacy Policy · effective November 17, 2026

Durak 3D is a card game you play on your own device. It does not need to know anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send any data off your device.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases and the Card Table Club subscription are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID; the game only learns from Apple which items you own, so it can unlock them.

What stays on your device

Your settings, World Tour and adventure progress, coins, achievements, daily tasks and an unfinished game are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Only the game can read them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
