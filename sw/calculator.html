Where do the prices come from?

Adult one-day lift ticket prices from OnTheSnow resort pages, weekday and weekend sample dates for 2026/27, checked November 2026. Where only last season's price was published it is marked °; where only an online "from" price exists it is marked ≈ and the window price is usually higher. Resorts change prices by date and demand, so treat the result as an estimate.

What does each pass cover?

Epic Pass ($1,145): unlimited at Vail, Beaver Creek, Breckenridge, Park City, Heavenly and the other Epic resorts, 7 days at Telluride. Epic Local ($849): unlimited at Breckenridge, Keystone, Crested Butte and the regional resorts, holiday restrictions at Park City, Heavenly, Northstar, Kirkwood and Stowe, 10 days in total at Vail and Beaver Creek. Ikon Pass ($1,449): unlimited at 18 destinations such as Winter Park, Copper, Steamboat, Mammoth and Palisades Tahoe, 7 days at the others. Ikon Base ($1,099): unlimited at 15, 5 days at most others, not valid at Jackson Hole, Alta, Snowbasin or Sun Valley; blackout dates Dec 26–30, Jan 16–17 and Feb 13–14. Sources: OnTheSnow buyer's guides and ikonpass.com, November 2026.

How are days beyond a limit counted?

Days beyond a pass limit, at resorts the pass does not cover, or on blackout dates, are counted at the full window price. Pass holders often get discounted tickets on those days, so your real cost may be a little lower.
