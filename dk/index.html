Durak 3D: World Tour

Support

Durak, the classic Russian card game, on a 3D table: podkidnoy and perevodnoy rules, a World Tour of 12 cities, story adventures and a puzzle of the day. The game plays fully offline.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: support@calmappslab.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

Does the game need the internet? No. Everything runs on your device.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

How do I cancel the Card Table Club subscription? In your Apple ID settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I bought something and it is missing. Write to us with the date of the purchase and we will help.
